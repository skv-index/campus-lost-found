Simple Process

Four steps to recovery

Designed to turn a frustrating lost-item scenario into a fast, verified return.

01

Report

Submit details and photo of a lost or found item. Describe key features, date, and precise campus location.

02

Search

Filter the live database by location, category, date, or unique tags to find matching campus listings instantly.

03

Match

Automated smart matching links lost reports with found item entries and notifies users via instant alerts.

04

Recover

Verify ownership via security questions or student ID, then collect your item at a designated campus office.
