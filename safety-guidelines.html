Designated Safe Handover Zones

All physical exchanges must take place within verified, monitored campus locations.

Central Security Desk

Main Administrative Building. Open 24/7 with CCTV surveillance.

Central Library Desk

Library Lobby Information Hub. Mon–Fri: 8:00 AM – 10:00 PM.

Student Union Office

Student Activity Building, Room 102. Mon–Fri: 9:00 AM – 5:00 PM.

02

High-Value Property Protocol

High-value items (market value exceeding $100) require mandatory turn-in to a registered Campus Security Custody Station within 24 hours of discovery.
